Plant vs. Animal Cell

Two expressions of the same fundamental unit — one builds walls and harvests light, the other moves and consumes.

Animal Cell


  • No cell wall — only a flexible membrane
  • Small, multiple vacuoles (if any)
  • No chloroplasts — relies on food
  • Irregular, fluid shifting shapes
  • Has centrioles & lysosomes
  • Medical Example 1: Human red blood cells transport oxygen throughout the body.
  • Medical Example 2: White blood cells protect the body by fighting bacteria and viruses.

Plant Cell


  • Rigid rectangular cell wall (cellulose)
  • Large central vacuole for storage & support
  • Chloroplasts for photosynthesis
  • Regular, fixed geometric shape
  • Usually does not have centrioles
  • Medical Example 1: Foxglove plants provide digoxin, a medicine used to treat heart failure.
  • Medical Example 2: Willow bark contains compounds that led to the development of aspirin.
